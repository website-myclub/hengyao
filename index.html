about HengYao Engineering Pte Ltd

 Heng Yao Engineering Pte Ltd is a Singapore-based engineering company established in 2015. We specialize in providing comprehensive engineering, fire protection, and renovation solutions tailored to commercial, industrial, and retail environments.

 With a strong commitment to quality, safety, and efficiency, we deliver reliable services that meet both client expectations and regulatory standards. Our experienced team is capable of managing projects of various scales, from small-scale renovations to large and technically demanding works. Over the years, we have built a solid reputation through our professionalism, timely delivery, and ability to provide one-stop solutions from design coordination and installation to testing, compliance, and maintenance.

 We strive to be a trusted partner for our clients by delivering consistent results and long-term value in every project we undertake.
